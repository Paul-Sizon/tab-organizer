    How Organize works

    ✨ Organize with AI looks at your open tabs, invents categories that actually fit them, then groups your tabs by those categories.

    Organize Tabs Now reuses your current category list — no AI round trip, so it's instant. Good for a quick re-sort once AI has already set things up.

    Already-sorted tabs are left alone — only new or changed tabs get reclassified.

    For privacy, AI receives only each tab's title, domain, and path — URL query parameters and fragments are removed first.
